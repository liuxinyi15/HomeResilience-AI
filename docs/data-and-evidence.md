# Data and evidence

| Source | What the release includes | What it excludes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Environment Agency [RoFRS](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/943d2bbb-aa08-45d1-96cb-42556cd01d94/risk-of-flooding-from-rivers-and-sea2), [present extents](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/1589b7ec-2300-4028-9199-aa7cac4b0f68/flood-map-for-planning-present-day-extents-defended-and-undefended), [depths](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/e4698ae1-184b-4b5d-801a-90364a93e229/rivers-and-sea-defended-and-undefended-flood-risk-depths-present-day), and [climate scenarios](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cc447d2e-2f27-44a1-b09b-e6d5c6fbf27b/rivers-and-sea-defended-and-undefended-flood-risk-extents-climate-change) | Thirteen processed York layer pairs for point analysis and simplified display, plus RoFRS map raster and legend. The [manifest](../site/data/processed/flood/manifest.json) records provenance, query bounds, counts and hashes. | Raw responses, national coverage, live warnings, surface water and groundwater. A complete bounded query does not imply complete flood risk coverage. |
| DESNZ [NEED impact tables](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/national-energy-efficiency-data-framework-need-impact-of-measures-data-tables-2026) and [2026 anonymised data](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/national-energy-efficiency-data-framework-need-anonymised-data-2026) | Extracted group effects and their source references, compact model lookup JSON, aggregate evaluation and limitations. The historical model uses the public 50,000-row sample. | Both the 50,000 and multi-million-row source files, workbooks, per-row predictions and training artifacts. |
| [Energy Performance of Buildings Data](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/) | Aggregate evaluation and subgroup counts, methodology, source and split hashes. Agreement with positive assessor references tests parts of the planning method. | Certificates, recommendation rows, addresses, postcodes, property IDs and property-level split or prediction data. Agreement does not establish savings or measure false recommendations. |
| Engineering and cost evidence | A source-linked intervention catalogue, selected published cost references, engineering checks and factual BSI configuration references. The app links to publisher records and labels illustrative values. | Proprietary standards, directory pages, certificates, reports, product logos and source PDFs. Always check a current listing and the surveyed property. |
| Demo fixtures and user inputs | Fictional household profiles, fictional quotes, and user-entered assumptions processed locally. | Customer records, live quotes, lender integrations and independent verification. |

The snapshot reproduces the **demo calculations and displayed evidence**, not the historical model training or access-controlled EPC evaluation pipeline. References to upstream filenames and source hashes in the reports document provenance; they do not point to files redistributed here.
