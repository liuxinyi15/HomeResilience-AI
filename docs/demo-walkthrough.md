# Emma’s York resilience journey

Emma is a fictional homeowner. Quote totals, installation references and maintenance dates in this walkthrough are synthetic. The product configurations come from the application’s offline BSI directory evidence. The risk and portfolio figures are calculated by the running app.

Open the hosted demo, or [serve the static site locally](../README.md#run-locally). Use the same origin for reloads. If the demo has been used before, select **Reset Emma demo** on Start and confirm. This restores the canonical £10,000 York fixture and clears only Emma’s records.

1. **Start.** Enable **Save my journey on this browser** and choose **Open Emma’s demo**. Understand shows a family semi in York, 110 m², EPC D, gas heating, and **High** mapped river and sea exposure. Open the Problem Map and point out the property checks that still need a survey.
2. **Plan.** Keep the £10,000 budget and central allowances. Open the suitability and evidence for a passive flood door and cavity insulation.
3. **Compare.** Recommended for your priorities is **£8,900** with a **£14,750** higher allowance. Best Value is **£6,100** with a **£9,800** higher allowance. The optimiser produces two distinct recommendations for these inputs. The cheaper option already represents the higher-cost-affordable alternative. Balance adds **£2,800** for self-closing air bricks, draught proofing and controls, and raised electrical services. Conditional coverage adds vents and services. The published group gas scenario stays the same. Choose **Recommended for your priorities**.
4. **Products & quotes.** Select **Load two fictional Emma quotes**. The table shows the documented PVCu and timber outward-opening configurations and the two synthetic totals of **£3,100** and **£2,350**. Quote B appears **£750** cheaper. The comparison identifies unknown VAT and survey scope, different removal scope and different configurations. Continue to funding with the selected Balance plan.
5. **Fund.** Choose **Staged retrofit**, keep **£2,000** available now and save the pathway. At central allowances the milestones are **£1,700** for the flood survey, air bricks and drain valve, **£6,100** cumulative after the flood door and raised electrical services, and **£8,900** cumulative after the remaining energy work. The walkthrough uses cumulative planning allowances. Request a phased quotation for repeat visits. For the finance example, enter £2,000 contribution, 60 months and 0% nominal annual interest. It shows **£6,900** financed, **£115/month**, and **£6,900** total repayments under those assumptions. The incentive route shows **Potentially relevant**, subject to provider checks.
6. **Deliver.** Review the project scope and installer documentation requirements. Select **Record work through Stage 1**.
7. **Verify.** Under **Self-closing air bricks**, record today’s date and provider `Emma demo installer`. Leave product and configuration blank for this generic measure. Save and observe **Installed**. Review without evidence to see **Verification incomplete**. Add a **Completion record** with reference `Emma demo completion` and an **Invoice** with reference `Emma demo invoice`. Check the review declaration and save it. The record now displays **Verified** under the demo’s declared document review.
8. **Passport.** Verify that the installation and evidence are visible. The previous unexecuted stages now appear in decision history because the recorded installation changes future suitability. The maintenance state is **No schedule available**.
9. **Maintain.** Open **Record a documented schedule** for air bricks. Enter `Fictional Emma handover schedule`, choose a date 14 days from today and confirm the source. The state becomes **Upcoming**. This is a fictional due date from the demo handover entry.
10. **Reassess.** Reload to demonstrate persistence. Open the Passport and choose **Reassess home**. The Problem Map lists the recorded air bricks. The recalculated options exclude a duplicate installation. The Passport retains the evidence and schedule.
11. **Show invalidation.** Confirm a freshly calculated option and save a funding route. In **Climate explorer**, select **Outside York pilot · Leeds**. Coverage becomes **Unknown**. Compare and Fund show **Outdated** until a new portfolio is confirmed. In Passport, the previous plan and pathway appear in **Decision history**. The completed installation remains recorded. Select **Export Passport JSON** to download the scoped record and see export feedback.

The automated reproduction is `node tests/journey-browser.cjs` with [Playwright available](architecture.md#reproduce-the-release-checks). It checks the quote delta, certified evidence integrity, staged costs, finance calculation, verification states, reload persistence and duplicate suppression on reassessment.
