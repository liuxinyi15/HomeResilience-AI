# Decision methodology

**Assessment.** The engine combines the selected pilot coordinate with Environment Agency modelled flood bands, scenario extents and depth evidence. Coverage gaps and explicitly unavailable depth stay unknown. Property details and reported completed work inform eligibility, survey needs and applicable insulation evidence. The map’s area result is not a diagnosis of an individual building.

**Recommendations.** The engine enumerates combinations of eligible measures and rejects sets that exceed the budget or break dependencies or incompatibility rules. A portfolio is evaluated on cost and flood, energy and comfort preferences. Balanced, Flood first and Energy first use different weights; the engine presents distinct alternatives, evaluates cost and benefit cases and shows higher-cost affordability. Flood scores represent conditional pathway coverage and comfort is an assumed proxy. Neither is a measured outcome.

**Energy.** For eligible gas-heated property groups, published observational NEED insulation effects are applied to an explicitly entered annual gas scenario or a 10,000 kWh reference. Combination cohorts are used where supported to avoid adding overlapping effects. Missing or incompatible evidence produces an unquantified outcome. The group quartiles describe household variation and are not prediction intervals. Retrospective consumption model outputs are separate from package ranking.

**Comparison and costs.** The editable Comparison Lab checks up to four plans against a chosen baseline. It recomputes changes in scope, dependencies, estimated cost ranges and evidence. Reported quotes are labelled separately from catalogue allowances; unknown or different scope prevents a like-for-like conclusion. A scoped quote only affects the confirmed decision after the revised plan is re-evaluated and selected. Product listings describe certified configurations, not installation suitability.

**Funding.** The staged route forms nested, dependency-valid subsets using the chosen plan’s evaluated costs. Phase totals are cumulative allowances. Illustrative monthly repayments use financed principal after cash contribution, the entered term, a fixed nominal annual rate divided by 12 and no fees; a zero rate divides principal by term. Grant signposts indicate possible relevance based on scope and geography and need a provider check.

**Verification and history.** Delivery and evidence records are user-entered. A recorded completion and declared document review serve different purposes. Maintenance uses a user-confirmed documented schedule. A material change to inputs or assumptions invalidates dependent decisions and keeps them in Passport history. Completed work stays recorded and is screened out of duplicate recommendations.
